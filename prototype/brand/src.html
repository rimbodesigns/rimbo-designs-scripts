The balls

Our pattern. Grainy circles where the orange fades through steel into beige. They used to go grey in the middle. Now they don't.

Before
Now

The fade, always in this order

  1. Deep Orange

  2. Hot Edge

  3. Steel, in the ball

  4. Beige, in the ball

On every ground

  • No grey

    Nothing grey between the colours. If a fade turns muddy, it's wrong.

  • Keep the edge

    Inside a ball the beige sits a step below the page, so the circle never melts into a beige ground.

  • Always grain

    A fine stipple over everything, like a riso print. Flat balls aren't ours.

  • They react

    On hover the orange floods the ball and fades back. Slow, one ball at a time.
